Where the community meets

For readers who want to build

The AI Operator Community is where readers of the AI Operator Series bring their questions and their work. Whether you have just finished your first chapter or you already run an agent in production, you can ask for help, show what you built and find people to work with.

Why join?

What the community is for, from reading about AI to building with it.

Peer discussion

Talk with founders, technical leads, no-code builders and engineers who build with AI. Compare approaches and find someone who has faced the same problem.

Questions about the books

Ask about a specific chapter, an implementation problem or a tool choice in the channel for that book, where other readers and the authors can see it.

Live sessions

Workshops, tool demos and Q&A sessions are announced on the Discord server when they are scheduled, with the date and the topic.

Shared templates

Members share their own automation workflows, prompt libraries and agent architectures. Read and test each one before you use it in your own work.

News on the books

The series has four books available and two in preparation. News about new books, chapters and companion resources is posted in the community.

Free to join

Joining costs nothing and you can leave at any time. All you need is a Discord account.

Community guidelines

A few principles that keep this community useful for everyone.

Ship, don’t just talk

We value action over theory. Share what you have built, what you are building and what you learned from what failed. Implementation stories beat abstract debates.

Help generously

If you have been through it, help someone who is going through it now. Answer questions, share resources and pass on the help you received when you started.

Stay curious

AI tools change quickly and what worked last month may not work today. Come to every conversation open to new approaches, tools and points of view.

No spam, no pitches

This is a space for learning and building, not selling. Keep promotion out of the discussion channels and focus on helping each other.
