No-Code AI Toolkit: pricing guide

Compare how the tools in The No-Code AI Bible charge: credits, tasks, workflow executions, API usage and app capacity. Use this guide to estimate your own workload, then check the linked official pages for current prices, limits and billing terms. Pricing models and source links reviewed on 9 October 2026.

Automation platforms

A credit, a task and a workflow execution are different billing units. Compare the cost of completing the same business process, including its exceptions and retries.

Automation

Make.com

Billing basis: plans include a credit allowance; module actions and some AI features consume credits. Budget check: run a representative scenario and inspect its credit usage, including repeated steps. Check scheduling, data transfer and extra-credit terms on the selected plan.

Automation

Zapier

Billing basis: task allowances depend on the plan; successful billable actions consume tasks, while triggers and some built-in steps do not. Budget check: count the billable actions per completed workflow and check what happens at the task limit, including any pay-per-task charges.

Zapier pricing (opens in a new tab)
Automation

n8n

Billing basis: paid plans use workflow executions, rather than charging separately for every step. Budget check: estimate runs, concurrency and log retention. For self-hosting, include infrastructure, maintenance and the features and licence your use requires; hosting it yourself does not remove operating costs.

AI platforms

Model API costs depend on usage and model choice. Estimate them separately from the automation platform subscription, and check charges for tools, caching and non-text inputs when relevant.

AI platform

OpenAI API

Billing basis: rates vary by model and input/output usage; caching, tools and audio or image processing can have their own charges. Budget check: measure a representative request, including its response and tool calls, and apply the rates for the exact model and features you use.

OpenAI pricing (opens in a new tab)
AI platform

Claude API (Anthropic)

Billing basis: input and output tokens have model-specific rates, with separate treatment for prompt caching, batch processing and some tools. Budget check: include document length, generated output, repeated context and tool use. Consult API pricing rather than assuming a chat subscription covers the workflow.

Claude pricing (opens in a new tab)
AI platform

Google Gemini

Billing basis: charges and free-tier availability vary by model and feature. Budget check: check input/output rates, caching, grounding and media processing for the selected model. Review rate limits and the data-use terms of the tier before using business data.

No-code app builders

The subscription is only part of the comparison. Check the users, data, workload and integrations your app needs, including limits that change between plans.

App builder

Bubble

Billing basis: project plans include workload capacity, measured in workload units; additional workload and other features can affect the bill. Budget check: measure your app's usage and review overage controls, storage and the plan needed to deploy the app.

App builder

Softr

What to compare: the plan that covers your app users, data sources and required permissions. Budget check: list who needs access and what they must be allowed to do, then confirm the included capacity, integration availability and upgrade conditions on the official plan comparison.

What to compare: the plan that supports your users, data sources, integrations and expected app activity. Budget check: review included users, updates and data limits, plus the conditions and costs of additional usage. Test with a representative app before committing to a billing period.

Check the official rate for your configuration

This guide explains cost drivers and links to the official pricing pages reviewed on 9 October 2026. It does not quote a fixed price for your setup. Confirm currency, taxes, selected usage allowance and monthly or annual billing before purchasing.

Tip

Measure one complete workflow

Test a small, representative sample with authorised data. Record successful outcomes, billable actions, model usage, retries and human review time. Multiply by your expected monthly volume, and include peaks and a budget for exceptions.

Tip

Compare monthly and annual billing

Compare the total commitment over the same period, including minimum seats or usage, add-ons, overages and renewal terms. A lower advertised monthly equivalent may require annual payment. Recheck the estimate when the workflow, model or volume changes.
