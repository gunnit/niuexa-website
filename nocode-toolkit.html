Automation platforms

The three no-code automation platforms covered in the book. Each has a free tier to start with; the differences matter as you scale.

Automation

Make.com

Free tier: 1,000 operations a month and 2 active scenarios, enough to learn and run simple automations. Pro from $9/mo: 10,000 operations, unlimited scenarios, priority execution. Suits visual builders who want fine control over how data flows.

Make.com pricing (opens in a new tab)
Automation

Zapier

Free tier: 100 tasks a month, 5 Zaps, single-step only. Limited, but fine for testing. Starter from $19.99/mo: 750 tasks, multi-step Zaps, filters and formatters. Suits teams that want the widest integration library and the simplest setup.

Zapier pricing (opens in a new tab)
Automation

n8n

Self-hosted: free to run on your own server or with Docker; you pay only for the hosting. Cloud from $20/mo: managed hosting, 2,500 executions, 5 active workflows. Suits technical users who want full control and a source-available tool.

AI platforms

The AI APIs behind your no-code automations. All are pay-per-use, and most offer a free tier or trial credits.

AI platform

OpenAI API

Pay-per-use: billed per token, roughly $0.002 to $0.06 per 1,000 tokens depending on the model. Smaller models such as GPT-5 nano cost far less than GPT-5. Suits users who want a wide choice of models and a mature API ecosystem.

OpenAI pricing (opens in a new tab)
AI platform

Claude API (Anthropic)

Pay-per-use: token-based pricing similar to OpenAI. Claude handles long documents well, with context windows of up to 1M tokens on some models. The free tier is in the claude.ai app, not the API. Suits careful analysis and long-document work.

Claude pricing (opens in a new tab)
AI platform

Google Gemini

Free tier: the Gemini API offers free usage with rate limits, the easiest place to start. Above those limits you pay per use. Text, image, video and audio input out of the box. Suits budget-conscious builders and multimodal use cases.

Gemini pricing (opens in a new tab)

No-code app builders

Build web and mobile applications without code. These platforms work with the automation tools above to make complete AI-powered tools.

App builder

Bubble

Free tier: build and test apps with Bubble branding. Starter from $29/mo: custom domain, no branding, basic analytics. The most flexible of the three, able to model complex SaaS-style products. Suits apps that need custom logic and database structures.

Bubble pricing (opens in a new tab)
App builder

Softr

Free tier: client portals and internal tools with Softr branding. Basic from $49/mo: custom domain, no branding, 5 internal users. Uses Airtable or Google Sheets as the back end. Suits internal tools, client portals and membership sites.

Softr pricing (opens in a new tab)
App builder

Glide

Free tier: apps built on Google Sheets with Glide branding, up to 500 rows. Starter from $25/mo: custom domain, 25,000 rows, integrations. Turns a spreadsheet into an app quickly. Suits teams that already work in Google Sheets.

Glide pricing (opens in a new tab)

Before you budget

Prices for no-code and AI tools change often. Three things to keep in mind when you read this guide.

Disclaimer

Prices last checked in March 2026

Vendors change prices and plan limits often, so always confirm on the vendor's pricing page before you buy. The buttons above link straight to each vendor's official pricing page.

Tip

Start with the free tiers

Every tool above has a free tier or a trial. Build your first automations there and upgrade only when you reach a limit.

Tip

Compare monthly and annual billing

The prices above are for monthly billing. Several vendors offer a discount for paying annually; check how large it is on their pricing page if you expect to use a tool long term.
