2. What data we process, why, and on what legal basis

2.1 Contact, quote and assessment requests

  • Data: what you enter in the form: first and last name, email and, depending on the form, phone number, company, role, number of employees, website, service of interest, indicative budget and message.
  • Where the visit came from: with the form we send the campaign UTM parameters found in the page address (for example utm_source and utm_campaign), the landing page, the page with the form and the page you came from (referrer). Google Ads click IDs (gclid, gbraid, wbraid) are added only if you have accepted “Marketing Cookies” (section 2.9).
  • Purpose: to answer your request, prepare a quote or an assessment and handle any negotiation that follows; to know which channel or campaign requests come from.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for the data on where the visit came from, our legitimate interest in knowing which channels bring in requests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); for click IDs, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • How it reaches us: the form sends your data to Web3Forms, a service that receives the submission and forwards it to Niuexa by email.

2.2 Event registrations

  • Data: first name, last name, company, email, mobile number and the date of the event you choose.
  • Purpose: to handle your registration request and send you practical information about the event.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • How it reaches us: through Web3Forms, which forwards the request by email. The event registration pages use no analytics tools and store nothing in your browser; registering does not sign you up for marketing communications.

2.3 Newsletter and guide resources

  • Newsletter: if you subscribe on the newsletter page, or tick the optional box in a form to receive updates and content about AI, we use your name and email address to send you the newsletter. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Guide resources: on the guide pages (the /books/ section) you can request a resource by leaving your name and email, which we use to send you that resource (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Receiving the newsletter as well requires your consent.
  • In both cases the data reaches us through Web3Forms, by email.

2.4 Email correspondence

  • Data: email address, the content of messages and attachments, and any contact details you give us.
  • Purpose: to reply to messages and manage relationships with clients, prospective clients and suppliers.
  • Legal basis: steps before entering into a contract or performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); otherwise our legitimate interest in replying to people who write to us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Provider: email for niuexa.ai runs on Google Workspace.

2.5 Clients: contracts and legal obligations

  • Data: contact details of the people we deal with, billing data and data about the service provided.
  • Purpose: to provide the agreed services, invoice them and meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

2.6 Running and securing the website

  • Data: IP address, date and time of the request, page requested, referring page, browser and operating system type.
  • Purpose: to deliver the pages to you, protect the site from abuse and attacks, and find errors.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Provider: the site is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), which processes this data on our behalf.

2.7 Files loaded from other providers

Some pages load files from third-party servers. When your browser requests them, the provider receives your IP address and the technical details of the request (browser type, referring page). These files do not set cookies.

  • Google Fonts (Google): most pages load their typefaces from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com); the event registration pages use typefaces hosted on our own site.
  • Google Tag Manager (Google): the script that manages the analytics and advertising measurement tags is loaded on every page except the event registration pages. The tags it contains follow the choices you make in the cookie banner (sections 2.8 and 2.9).
  • jsDelivr (Volentio JSD Limited, United Kingdom): the ROI calculator loads its charting library from cdn.jsdelivr.net.
  • AEO Analyzer: the Italian home page loads a script from our AEO Analyzer service, hosted on Render (Render Services, Inc., United States). The script applies technical search-engine optimisations to the page and records which ones it applied, with the page address and the browser type.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in presenting a consistent, readable and working site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time (section 7).

2.8 Usage statistics (Google Analytics 4)

  • With your consent to the “Analytics Cookies” category in the banner, Google Analytics 4 sets the _ga and _ga_8JW0S3PJKM cookies and collects: pages visited; actions on the site, such as clicks on buttons and links, scrolling, opening and submitting forms, completing a certification; device and browser data; approximate location derived from the IP address; referring page and campaign parameters. We do not send Google Analytics your name, email or anything else you type into our forms.
  • Without consent Google's tags still load, but under Google Consent Mode they neither set nor read cookies and use no persistent identifiers. They may send Google cookieless signals (for example the type of event, the time, the browser type and the consent status), which Google uses for aggregate statistical estimates.
  • Purpose: to understand how the site is used, so we can improve its content and how it works.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code); for the cookieless signals, our legitimate interest in aggregate measurement of how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Provider: Google Ireland Limited, which processes Google Analytics data on our behalf.

2.9 Advertising campaign measurement

With your consent to the “Marketing Cookies” category in the banner we use the tools listed here. Without that consent LinkedIn and Apollo.io are not loaded, we store no attribution data in your browser, and the Google Ads tags use no cookies: they can only send the cookieless signals described in section 2.8.

  • Request attribution (Niuexa): when you arrive from a campaign we store in your browser (localStorage, key niuexa_attribution_v1) the UTM parameters, the Google Ads click IDs (gclid, gbraid, wbraid), the landing page and the referring page and, if you submit a form, the form name and the time it was sent. If you submit a form on a later visit, this data is added to the request so we know which campaign it came from. We keep it for at most 90 days from its last update.
  • Google Ads (Google Ireland Limited): the Conversion Linker and the conversion tag measure how many requests come from ads, using the _gcl_au, _gcl_aw and _gcl_gb cookies.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag (LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company): measures conversions from LinkedIn campaigns and provides aggregate statistics about the site's audience, using LinkedIn cookies.
  • Apollo.io (ZenLeads, Inc. d/b/a Apollo.io, United States): records the pages visited, the referring page and the UTM parameters and links them to a random identifier stored in your browser, to recognise the companies, and where possible the people, visiting the site. Where the service allows it, Apollo's script also loads a LiveIntent script (United States), which can match the visit to hashed email addresses already known to LiveIntent; these hashes are sent to Apollo together with the visit data.
  • Purpose: to measure how well advertising campaigns work and link the requests we receive to the campaigns they came from; with Apollo.io, to identify companies interested in our services for our sales activity.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code), which you can withdraw at any time (section 7).

2.10 YouTube videos

The Italian Consulenza AEO e GEO page contains a video hosted on YouTube (Google Ireland Limited). Until you press play or accept “Marketing Cookies”, the page shows only a panel with the video title and does not contact YouTube. The video is then loaded from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode: from that point Google receives your IP address and browser data, and YouTube may use its own cookies and other storage under its own privacy policy. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code), which you give by accepting “Marketing Cookies” or by pressing play after reading the notice below the panel.

2.11 Data that stays in your browser

  • Certifications: the certificate is generated in your browser. The name, email, company and LinkedIn profile you enter stay saved on your device and are not sent to us. With your consent to “Analytics Cookies”, Google Analytics records only that a certification was completed, with the tutorial and the score.
  • ROI calculator and tutorials: the values you enter in the calculator and the tutorials you bookmark stay in your browser.

You can delete this data at any time in your browser settings. The full list is in the Cookie Policy.

3. Do you have to provide your data?

No. In our forms only the fields we need to answer your request are required, and they are marked as such: without them we cannot act on the request, but you can always email us instead. The event registration form requires all five fields. Subscribing to the newsletter and consenting to “Analytics Cookies” and “Marketing Cookies” are optional: if you decline, you can use the site and its forms without any restriction.

6. How long we keep data

  • Contact, quote and assessment requests, resource requests and event registrations: for as long as needed to handle the request and any negotiation that follows.
  • Clients' contract and accounting data: 10 years from the end of the relationship.
  • Email correspondence: for as long as needed to manage the relationship it concerns, on the same criteria.
  • Newsletter: until you withdraw your consent.
  • Browsing data collected with Google Analytics: 24 months at most.
  • Technical data processed by Cloudflare to run and secure the site: only for as long as strictly needed for those purposes.
  • Campaign attribution stored in your browser (niuexa_attribution_v1): 90 days at most from its last update. It is deleted when you refuse or withdraw consent to “Marketing Cookies”.
  • Cookies and other technologies: for the durations given in the Cookie Policy.

7. Your rights

At any time you can ask for:

  • access to your data and a copy of it (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR);
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • portability, that is, to receive the data you gave us in a structured format and pass it to another controller (Art. 20 GDPR).

You also have the right to object to processing based on our legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation, and at any time to processing for direct marketing (Art. 21 GDPR).

You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal (Art. 7(3) GDPR):

  • for analytics and marketing cookies and similar tools, with the “Manage Cookies” button in the Cookie Policy, which clears your saved choice and shows the banner again;
  • for the newsletter, with the unsubscribe link in the emails or by writing to privacy@niuexa.ai.

To exercise your rights, write to privacy@niuexa.ai. We reply within one month of your request; this can be extended by two further months for complex requests (Art. 12 GDPR).

If you believe the processing of your data breaches the GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it), or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live, work or where the alleged breach took place (Art. 77 GDPR).

8. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR).
