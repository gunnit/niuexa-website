A three-phase path with shared goals

Every project starts from an operational need and moves through verifiable phases, with clear goals and responsibilities from day one.

  1. 01

    Phase 01

    Use case analysis

    We map the process, the people involved, the available data and the constraints. Together we define the problem to solve and how to judge the outcome.

    Result: shared priorities, feasibility and success criteria

  2. 02

    Phase 02

    Solution design

    We design flows, integrations and human checks that fit your systems, taking into account security, data permissions and how people will use it.

    Result: a defined solution, explicit requirements and a work plan

  3. 03

    Phase 03

    Implementation and adoption

    We build and verify the solution in the agreed context, document how it works and support the people who will use it every day.

    Result: a verified solution, documentation and team support

Four choices that guide every project

The same rules apply to every project, from the first call to delivery.

  • The process before the tool

    We choose the technology only after understanding tasks, volumes, exceptions and owners. That way the solution solves a real problem.

  • Human oversight and measurement

    Before we start, we define what to measure and which steps stay with a person. Clear criteria, verified together, instead of promises.

  • Integration with your systems

    No need to replace your CRM, ERP or management software: where it makes sense, we connect the solution to the tools your team already uses.

  • Italian team in Milan and Turin

    Meet us in person or remotely, with attention to GDPR and the AI Act at every stage: from the data you use to the controls you adopt.

The questions we get before starting

Straight answers on timing, costs, data and integrations. For everything else, there's the first call.

What happens after I submit the form?

A Niuexa consultant reads your request and replies within 24 business hours to schedule the first 30-minute call. During the call we evaluate your use case together and define the next steps. If it doesn't make sense to go ahead, we'll tell you.

How much does it cost? How does the quote work?

The first call is free and without commitment. Costs depend on the scope of the project: number of processes, integrations, security requirements and training. We define them after the analysis, in a written quote, so you know upfront what is included.

Do we need lots of data or a large budget to get started?

Not necessarily. Niuexa projects start from a well-defined use case, often with the data your company already has in emails, documents and management systems. In the first call we check what you have available and whether it's enough for a useful first step, before talking about investment.

How long does a project take?

There is no standard timeline: it depends on scope, data quality, integrations, security requirements and testing. The duration is defined after the use case analysis, together with the work plan, without promising dates we can't verify.

How do you handle data security and GDPR?

From the analysis onward we define which data is needed, who can access it and where it is processed. We design the flows with attention to GDPR and the AI Act: data permissions, traceability of operations and a human checkpoint on sensitive decisions.

Can you work with the software we already use?

Yes, where it makes sense. There is no need to replace your CRM, ERP or management software: we integrate the solution into your existing flows and systems through APIs and automation, limiting unnecessary changes. We verify this together during the analysis phase.
