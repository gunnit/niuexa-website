What to expect

One topic per evening

Each meeting goes into one subject: visibility in AI answers, AI agents on business processes, AI agents in marketing.

Operational questions

Which process to start with, who decides, what to measure before and after. The talk is about the real constraints of a business, not about products.

Meeting in person

The meetings take place in Milan, in person, so there is room to talk with people facing the same questions in other companies.
