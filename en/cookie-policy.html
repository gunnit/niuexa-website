Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This page lists the cookies and similar technologies used on niuexa.ai: who uses them, what they are for, which banner category they belong to and how long they last. The data controller is NIUEXA S.R.L.; how we process personal data is explained in the Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. The browser's local storage (localStorage), which keeps data until it is deleted, and session storage (sessionStorage), which is cleared when you close the tab, work in a similar way.

In line with the Italian data protection authority's (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) guidelines of 10 June 2021, technical tools that the site needs to work, or that a feature you ask for needs, do not require consent; analytics and marketing tools are activated only with your consent.

2. How we handle consent

On your first visit a banner asks you to choose: “Accept All”, “Only Necessary” or “Customize”, to decide separately on the “Analytics Cookies” and “Marketing Cookies” categories. Until you choose, and if you decline, only technical tools are saved in your browser, unless you start the YouTube video on the AI Readiness Tool page (section 5). Your choice is also applied to Google's tags through Google Consent Mode: without consent Google's tags stay loaded but use no cookies (see section 2.8 of the Privacy Policy).

You can change your choice at any time with the “Manage Cookies” button at the bottom of this page: your saved choice is cleared and the banner appears again. To delete cookies that are already set, use your browser settings.

3. Technical tools (always active)

These are needed for the site to work or for features you use, and are saved only in your browser.

  • niuexa_cookie_consent (localStorage, Niuexa): remembers your cookie choices. Duration: until you change them or clear your browser data.
  • niuexa_certifications (localStorage, Niuexa): keeps the certificates you generate on the Certification page (name, email, company, LinkedIn profile, tutorial, score and date). Duration: until you clear your browser data.
  • roiCalculatorData (localStorage, Niuexa): remembers the values you enter in the ROI calculator. Duration: until you reset the calculator or clear your browser data.
  • niuexa_bookmarks (localStorage, Niuexa): remembers the tutorials you bookmark. Duration: until you remove them or clear your browser data.

Legal basis: no consent required (Art. 122(1) of the Italian Privacy Code); the processing relies on your request or our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

4. Analytics (only with consent to “Analytics Cookies”)

  • _ga (cookie, Google Analytics 4, Google Ireland Limited): distinguishes visitors. Duration: 2 years.
  • _ga_8JW0S3PJKM (cookie, Google Analytics 4, Google Ireland Limited): keeps the session state. Duration: 2 years.
  • niuexa_lead_event_… (sessionStorage, Niuexa): on the thank-you page, stops the same request from being counted twice if you reload the page. Duration: until you close the tab.

Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code).

5. Marketing (only with consent to “Marketing Cookies”)

  • niuexa_attribution_v1 (localStorage, Niuexa): UTM parameters, Google Ads click IDs (gclid, gbraid, wbraid), landing and referring page and the last form submitted, to link requests to campaigns. Duration: 90 days at most from its last update; deleted when you refuse or withdraw consent.
  • _gcl_au (cookie, Google Ads, Google Ireland Limited): measures ad conversions. Duration: 90 days.
  • _gcl_aw and _gcl_gb (cookies, Google Ads, Google Ireland Limited): keep the ad click identifier so a conversion can be attributed to it. Duration: 90 days.
  • LinkedIn cookies, for example li_fat_id, bcookie, lidc, UserMatchHistory and AnalyticsSyncHistory (LinkedIn Insight Tag, LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company): measure conversions from LinkedIn campaigns and provide aggregate statistics about the site's audience. Duration: from 1 day to 1 year; the current list is in LinkedIn's Cookie Policy.
  • apolloAnonId and 69b932d7754554002177ec54_eventQueue (localStorage, Apollo.io, ZenLeads, Inc.): random browser identifier and queue of visits to be sent to Apollo. Duration: until you clear your browser data.
  • 69b932d7754554002177ec54_canTrack and liveIntentData (localStorage, Apollo.io, ZenLeads, Inc.): result of the service check and hashed email codes received from LiveIntent. Duration: 24 hours.
  • LiveIntent technologies (LiveIntent): the LiveIntent script loaded by Apollo may use its own cookies and identifiers, under its own privacy policy.
  • YouTube cookies (Google Ireland Limited): the video on the Italian AI Readiness Tool page is loaded from youtube-nocookie.com only if you have accepted “Marketing Cookies” or press play on the video panel; from then on YouTube may use its own cookies and other storage, under its own privacy policy.

Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code).

6. Services that receive data without setting cookies

When you open some pages, your browser downloads files from these providers' servers, which receive your IP address and the technical details of the request but set no cookies: Google Fonts (most pages), Google Tag Manager (every page except the event registration pages), jsDelivr (ROI calculator) and our AEO Analyzer service on Render (Italian home page). The details are in the Privacy Policy.

7. What happens if you decline

The site and its forms work normally. If you decline “Marketing Cookies”, we cannot link your request to a campaign that brought you to the site on an earlier visit; if you decline “Analytics Cookies”, our statistics on how the site is used are less precise.

8. Managing cookies in your browser

In your browser settings you can view, block or delete cookies and other data saved by websites. If you block all site data, your browser cannot remember your choice and the banner appears on every visit.

9. Updates

We update this page when the tools used on the site change; the date at the top shows the last change.
