What the agent does, in detail

Six functions working on the same stream of data, from the first piece of content to the end-of-campaign report.

  • AI content generation

    Drafts blog posts, social posts, emails and video scripts in your brand's voice, from the sources you authorise: brand guidelines, published content, product sheets.

  • Multichannel campaigns

    Runs publishing and campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Google Ads, within the budgets and approval rules agreed with your team.

  • Measured results

    Before we start we set the baseline: production time, cost per piece of content, campaign results. After the first period we measure again with the same criteria.

  • Audience segmentation

    Segments the audience by behaviour and preferences and updates the segments at every interaction rather than every quarter, within the consents you have collected.

  • Trend estimates

    Forecasts of trends and audience behaviour to steer the budget before the month's spend has gone. They are estimates: decisions stay with your team.

  • Continuous A/B tests

    Tests variants continuously and flags the weaker ones, keeping your brand's voice and guidelines on every version.

We measure first, then automate

We do not publish result percentages: they depend on your process. Before we start we agree the baseline with you; after the first period we measure again with the same criteria and decide together whether to extend, correct or stop.

  • Production time

    Hours from brief to approved draft, for each format and channel.

  • Cost per piece

    Team hours and tool costs, divided by the content published.

  • Revisions

    How many drafts come back, and why.

  • Campaign results

    The metrics your team already uses, read on the same platforms.

The metrics are agreed in writing with the AI Metric Contract, a free worksheet (in Italian) you can also use with other vendors.
