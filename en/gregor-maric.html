Who is Gregor Maric

CEO and Co-Founder of Niuexa

Gregor Maric is an Italian entrepreneur working in automation and artificial intelligence. He co-founded Niuexa with Roberto Botto to make generative AI applicable to real business processes rather than pilots that never leave the lab.

His work centres on three areas: assessing which business processes can be automated with a measurable return, designing AI agents that integrate with the systems a company already runs (CRM, ERP, document management), and training the internal teams who will use them day to day.

His position on AI adoption is deliberately pragmatic: artificial intelligence does not replace people, it extends what they can do. Niuexa projects therefore start from a single scoped, measurable use case, with human-in-the-loop checks on the steps that matter.
