Who is Gregor Maric

CEO and co-founder of Niuexa

Gregor Maric has worked in business process automation since 2012: document workflows, RPA and, today, AI agents. He founded Niuexa with Roberto Botto to bring the same approach to SMEs: start from the process, measure it, and use AI only where it is needed.

At Niuexa he leads the automation projects and the training. The work always starts from one process at a time: he measures it as it is, automates it on the systems the company already uses (CRM, ERP, document management) and measures it again with the same criteria.

The principle he brings to every project is simple: the agent proposes, the person decides. Exceptions go to a person, never straight into the ERP. And when AI is not needed, he says so.

Questions about Gregor Maric

Who is Gregor Maric?

Gregor Maric is CEO and co-founder of Niuexa (NIUEXA S.R.L.), an AI consultancy with its registered office in Turin and an office in Milan. He has worked in business process automation since 2012: document workflows, RPA and, today, AI agents.

Who founded Niuexa?

Niuexa was founded by Gregor Maric, CEO, and Roberto Botto, founder and CEO of Libera Brand Building Group, the company that controls Niuexa.

What does Gregor Maric do at Niuexa?

He leads the automation projects and the training. Every project starts from one process at a time: it is measured as it is, automated on the systems the company already uses, measured again, and then the client and Niuexa decide together whether to extend, correct or stop. Exceptions go to a person: the agent proposes, the person decides.

What books has Gregor Maric written?

Gregor Maric is the author of The AI Operator Series, which includes The MCP Blueprint, The One-Person Empire, The No-Code AI Bible and The AI Agents Field Guide: four practical ebooks on implementing AI systems and agents.

Where can I follow Gregor Maric?

On the Niuexa Champion YouTube channel (@NiuexaChampion) and on his LinkedIn profile (maricgregor). Guides and articles are published on niuexa.ai.

How can I contact Gregor Maric?
