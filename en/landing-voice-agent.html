What the voice agent does

It answers the most frequent calls, collects the data needed and leaves anything outside the agreed rules to a person. We configure it on your customer service, not on a generic script.

  • Natural conversation

    Understands questions in the caller's own words, asks for clarification when needed and answers by voice, with no keypad menus. Answers are based on the information you authorise: opening hours, procedures, order status.

  • Several languages

    It can answer in Italian and in other languages the model supports. Which languages to enable, and at what quality, is tested on trial calls before going live.

  • Hours and volumes

    It can answer outside office hours and handle several calls at once. Hours covered, volumes and cases are agreed in the project and measured before and after.

The decisions that stay with a person

The agent proposes, the person decides. When a call requires changing customer data, such as an order, an appointment or a customer record, the agent prepares the change and a person confirms it before it enters the CRM or the ERP.

  • Transfer to an operator

    If the caller asks, if the request falls outside the rules or if the agent is not sure, the call goes to a person with a summary of what was said.

  • Tone of the conversation

    If the caller is unhappy or the conversation gets complicated, the agent flags it and transfers sooner instead of insisting.

  • CRM and phone system

    Connects to the CRM and phone system you already use when they offer an API or call forwarding. We check this in the analysis, before any quote.

We measure first, then automate

We do not publish result percentages: they depend on your customer service. Before we start we agree the baseline with you; after the first period we measure again with the same criteria and decide together whether to extend, correct or stop.

  • Calls handled

    How many requests the agent closes without a transfer, by call reason.

  • Waiting time

    Seconds before the call is answered, at peak times and outside office hours.

  • Transfers

    How many calls go to an operator and why, so the rules can be corrected.

  • CRM records

    How many requests reach the CRM complete and correct.

The metrics are agreed in writing with the AI Metric Contract, a free worksheet (in Italian) you can also use with other vendors.
