Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a reply?

We reply within 24 working hours of your request, to set up the first call at a time that suits you.

Is the first call free?

Yes. It lasts 30 minutes, is free and comes with no obligation: we look at one process together and tell you whether AI can lighten it. If you decide to go ahead, costs are set in a written quote after the analysis.

Do you work with small and medium-sized businesses?

Yes, we work mostly with Italian SMEs, one process at a time and on the systems the company already uses. The industry matters less than the process: volumes, regularity and exceptions decide whether automation makes sense.

Where do you operate?

We work across Italy from our registered office in Turin and our office in Milan. The first call and much of the work can be done remotely.
