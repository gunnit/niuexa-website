How we work

The process before the tool

We map activities, volumes, times and exceptions and set a starting measure. We choose the technology only afterwards, and measure again with the same criteria.

The agent proposes, the person decides

Exceptions and sensitive decisions go to a person, never straight into the ERP. Human control is part of the project, not an add-on.

On the systems you already use

There is no need to replace your CRM, ERP or business software: where it is sustainable, we connect the solution to the workflows and tools your team uses every day.

When AI is not needed, we say so. Sometimes a rule in the ERP, a better form or some order in the data is enough.

Our principles

Measurement

Every project starts from a measure of the process as it is and is judged on the same criteria at the end. We do not promise percentages up front.

Transparency

We say clearly what a solution does and does not do, and we put the cost in writing in a quote after the analysis.

Accountability

Decisions stay with the company's people. We design the human checks together with the people who will use the solution every day.

Shared knowledge

Free guides and worksheets, such as the AI Metric Contract and the AI Outcome Budget (in Italian), that you can use without us.
