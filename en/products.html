Keel

An MCP layer for Amazon Seller and Vendor

Keel connects Amazon Seller Central, Vendor Central, SP-API, Ads API and Brand Analytics to AI agents such as Claude and ChatGPT through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). You can query Amazon data in plain language, combining accounts, brands and marketplaces in one conversation. It includes seasonal forecasting, exports (Excel, CSV, Google Sheets, PDF, Notion), multi-currency reconciliation, per-agent permissions and a log of every query. It is built for sellers, vendors, multi-account agencies and e-commerce teams who want to stop exporting CSV files by hand.

Frequently asked questions

Timelines, requirements, customisation, support, security and cost of the AI agents.

How long does implementation take?

Timelines depend on the process, data, integrations, permissions, checks and tests. After the analysis we set a roadmap with milestones and go/no-go criteria, without promising a standard duration before we know the scope.

  • Analysis: process, starting measurement, data, integrations, owners and constraints.
  • Pilot: representative sample, quality criteria, errors and exceptions.
  • Go-live: only after the agreed checks are passed.
  • Operations: owner, monitoring, escalation and review schedule are documented.

How much does an AI agent cost?

The first 30-minute call is free. The cost of an agent depends on the process, volumes, integrations and service level, and is set in a written quote after the analysis. ChatAziendale, instead, has monthly plans published on its own site.

What are the minimum technical requirements?

Requirements change with the use case and the systems involved. We check them before the pilot, without assuming that every solution is cloud-only or needs no change to the existing architecture.

  • Authorised, up-to-date data sources with an identified owner
  • APIs, connectors or import procedures that are available and testable
  • Identities, roles, permissions and a test environment separate from production
  • Logs, monitoring, escalation and a continuity plan suited to the risk

Can the agents be customised?

Yes. Each agent is configured on the company's processes, approval rules, tone of voice and systems. Before building custom features we check inputs, outputs, integrations, permissions and exceptions; we do not build what is not needed.

  • Tone of voice: your brand's language and terminology.
  • Business processes: existing approval flows and decision rights.
  • Custom features: when the standard does not cover the use case.
  • Integrations: ERP, CRM, internal databases and existing software via APIs or connectors.

What kind of support is included?

Support is defined for each solution and the agreed service level. The quote spells out channels, hours, priorities, responsibilities and response times.

  • Agreed channels and availability window
  • Request classification and escalation path
  • An operational owner and review schedule
  • Monitoring, logs and the evidence needed for diagnosis
  • Training and documentation when included in the scope

How is data security checked?

Security is assessed on the actual scope of the solution and the vendors involved. Before go-live we document which data is processed, where it travels, who can access it and what evidence is available.

  • Data, purpose, location, retention and sub-processors
  • Encryption, identity, access control and separate environments
  • Logs, backups, incident response and business continuity
  • Deletion, export and exit clauses

Is there a trial period?

It depends on the solution. When useful, we set up a demo or a limited pilot to check the use case before extending the scope.

  • Sample or anonymised data consistent with the use case
  • Starting measurement and KPIs defined before the run
  • Normal cases, errors, exceptions and escalations included in the test
  • A documented final decision: extend, correct or stop

Is your website visible to AI assistants?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity answer with only a few sources. The free AI-readiness analysis checks how they read and cite your site and lists the priority fixes on content and structured data.
