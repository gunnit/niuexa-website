What we analyze for AI discovery

From SEO to AEO (Answer Engine Optimization): the audit evaluates your site’s technical and editorial readiness. Citations in AI answers and commercial results require separate measurements.

AI discovery signals

Checks the files and access settings that describe your site to crawlers and compatible tools.

  • llms.txt: a proposed convention for giving some tools a site summary; it does not replace robots.txt or guarantee citations
  • robots.txt: instructions for AI crawlers
  • API endpoints: machine-readable access

Semantic structure

How well the semantic structure and content of your site can be understood by AI assistants.

  • Schema.org: structured data present and correct
  • Entity recognition: who you are and what you offer
  • Knowledge graph: semantic connections

Site readiness score

Diagnostic scores based on site checks help compare two audits. They do not measure how often AI assistants cite your brand.

  • Score from 0 to 100: results of technical and editorial checks
  • Observed citations: checked separately, recording questions, platforms, dates and sources
  • Traffic and enquiries: measured through analytics and the enquiries received

Content quality

How well your content answers the questions people ask AI assistants.

  • Question Matching: question and answer structure
  • Intent Coverage: coverage of questions
  • Content Clarity: clarity for AI

Actionable guidance

A report with a tailored llms.txt file and an AEO roadmap with clear priorities.

  • llms.txt generator: a ready-to-use file
  • AEO strategy: an action plan with priorities
  • Quick fixes: simple changes you can make now

Competitor comparison

How AI assistants describe your competitors and where your site has gaps to close.

  • Who gets cited: the companies assistants name today
  • Gap analysis: what your site is missing
  • Priorities: where to start

Example improvements, to verify on the site

  1. High priority Make important pages accessible

    If a useful page is blocked by mistake, check robots.txt, the HTTP response and indexing directives. Correcting the issue allows crawling but does not guarantee indexing or citations.

  2. Medium priority Complete the answers to customer questions

    Describe services, intended customers, limits and verifiable evidence. Link each material claim to the source or project that supports it.

  3. Quick fix Align previews and structured data with the content

    Titles, descriptions and markup should reflect the page and its purpose. There is no special markup that guarantees inclusion in AI answers.

Is the analysis invasive for my site?

No. The analysis reads only public pages, with a crawler that does not overload your server and respects robots.txt and crawling rules. We do not change any file or content, and the analysis does not affect your site's performance.

What does the report include?

The free analysis returns a PDF or HTML report on your homepage, with readiness scores and a grade across five dimensions (technical, structure, content, performance, media) and recommendations ranked by impact. Business covers up to 20 agreed pages with a quarterly comparison of three competitors; Enterprise covers an agreed sample of up to 25 pages with a monthly comparison of three competitors. Extensions are defined in the quote. Both add an action plan and tracking over time. Audit scores do not measure citations in AI responses.

Do you need access to my site's backend?

Not for the analysis: we work only with publicly accessible information and do not ask for credentials or FTP access. For deeper analysis, if you wish, we can connect Google Analytics. In Enterprise, to apply changes, we need secure access to your CMS or hosting, and every change goes live only after your approval.

Can I apply the fixes myself?

Yes. The report is written to be applied: each recommendation comes with instructions and links to resources and tools, and the priority order shows where to start. If you want support alongside you, Business adds an action plan and email support.

Does the analysis guarantee that ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity will cite my company?

No. No one can guarantee a mention, a position or a volume of traffic: answers depend on the models and change over time. The analysis identifies technical and editorial issues and lets you compare audit scores. Citations must be checked separately in responses to documented questions, recording the platforms, dates and sources.
