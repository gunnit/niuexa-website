How we work

The method we offer clients is the one we work with every day, on real processes in small and medium Italian businesses.

Measure first

Every project starts by mapping the process as it is: volumes, times, exceptions, who decides. The tool comes later.

On existing systems

AI agents are built on the ERP, CRM and management software the client already uses, without asking them to switch platforms.

The person decides

The agent proposes, the person decides: exceptions go to whoever runs the process, never straight into the ERP or CRM.

Measure again

After release the process is measured again, and together with the client we decide to extend, correct or stop.

When AI is not needed

If a problem is better solved without AI, we tell the client. That is as much part of the job as building an agent.

Shared knowledge

Guides, free worksheets and the SIGNALS meetings are open to everyone, including people who never become clients.

Our principles

Four rules that apply to every project and to everyone who works on it.

Clarity

Costs and timing are put in writing in a quote, after the process has been analysed and not before.

No promises

We do not guarantee results: we agree with the client what to measure, how and when.

Collaboration

We work with the people who run the process every day, not only with whoever commissioned the project.

Learning

We try tools before proposing them, and what we learn goes into our guides and meetings.

Who we look for

You do not need years in AI. What counts is how you think about processes and that you want to keep learning.

Interest in processes

  • Curiosity about how an accounts office, a purchasing office or a sales team really works
  • Interest in applied AI, including the cases where it is not needed
  • A habit of trying tools before recommending them

Method

  • A habit of measuring before and after a change
  • Independence when facing open problems
  • Clarity when explaining technical choices to non-technical people

Wanting to learn

  • Openness to continuous learning
  • Willingness to change your mind when the data contradicts a hypothesis
  • Interest in specialising in AI applied to SME processes
