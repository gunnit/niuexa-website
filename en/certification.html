Quizzes on the Niuexa guides

After reading one of the free guides, check what you understood with a quiz of 10 to 15 questions. Score at least 80% and you can generate a Niuexa certificate of completion. It records that you passed an online quiz: it is not an accredited certification or a recognised qualification. The questions are in Italian.

  • 4guides with a quiz
  • 80%for the certificate
  • 10-15questions per quiz

Choose a guide

Each quiz covers one free guide. If you have not read it yet, you will find it in Learn.

How it works

Questions on the guide

Multiple choice and true or false questions on the topics of the guide. You can go back and change an answer before you submit.

Certificate at 80%

With at least 80% correct answers you can generate a PNG certificate with your name, the guide, your score and the date.

Download and share

Download the certificate and share your result on LinkedIn, X or WhatsApp.

Your data stays in your browser

Your name and email are only used to build the certificate. The form does not send them to Niuexa; they stay in your browser.
