The AI Operator Series

Four books in English by Gregor Maric on putting AI to work: connecting models to company systems, running a business on your own with AI, automating without code and running agents in production. This page is for readers: every book has a free companion page with the templates and checklists its chapters refer to.

Four books, four kinds of reader

Each book stands alone and is written for a different reader: technical leads who connect AI to company systems, founders who run a business alone, operators who automate without writing code, and teams that put agents into production. Two more titles are planned; their release date has not been announced.

If you have a copy, the QR codes in the chapters lead to the companion pages linked below. They hold the templates, checklists, updates and errata for each book, and they are free.

Readers also have a community on Discord: write to ai@niuexa.ai for an invite.
