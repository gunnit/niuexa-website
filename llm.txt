# Niuexa: AI Consulting, Process Automation and AI Visibility (AEO/GEO)

> Niuexa (NIUEXA S.R.L., VAT IT13489560014) is an AI consultancy for Italian SMEs,
> with its registered office in Turin (Via Vittorio Andreis 18) and an office in
> Milan (Via Rutilia 10). Founded in 2026 by Gregor Maric (CEO, in business process
> automation since 2012) and Roberto Botto, it is controlled by Libera Brand
> Building Group and is a member of AUSED, the association of Italian CIOs.
> Niuexa works on three fronts: process automation with AI agents built on the
> systems a company already uses, visibility in AI assistants (AEO/GEO), and
> practical AI training.

## How Niuexa works
- "Prima misuriamo, poi automatizziamo": map and measure one process as it is (volumes, times, errors, exceptions), automate it on the existing systems, measure again, then decide with the client whether to extend, correct or stop
- "L'agente propone, la persona decide": exceptions and sensitive decisions go to a person, never straight into the ERP or CRM
- Niuexa says so when AI is not needed
- No published prices: a project's cost is set in a written quote after the analysis. The first 30-minute call is free
- No guarantees of savings, ROI or rankings; results are measured against a baseline agreed in writing
- Free worksheets usable without hiring Niuexa: [AI Metric Contract](https://niuexa.ai/ai-metric-contract.html), [AI Outcome Budget](https://niuexa.ai/ai-outcome-budget.html), [12 domande prima di firmare con un fornitore AI](https://niuexa.ai/articolo-due-diligence-fornitori-ai-pmi.html)
