An Example Sales Pipeline

A possible sequence to adapt to your own tools, contacts, and sales process.

Stage 1

Capture & Score

Capture a lead through an approved source, record the available details, and apply agreed qualification criteria. Keep unknown information explicit and review uncertain scores.

Stage 2

Nurture & Qualify

Prepare relevant follow-up drafts using documented information. Treat engagement signals as evidence to review, not proof of buying intent, and send only through an approved process.

Stage 3

Propose & Confirm

Propose a meeting when the agreed conditions are met, obtain approval, and confirm the booking result. A proposal draft remains subject to human review; this workflow does not guarantee a closed deal.
